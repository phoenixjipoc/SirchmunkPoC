The system is not a department or agency of the state but is an independent instrumentality exercising essential government functions. INPRS was established by legislation to manage the retirement funds of certain public employees throughout the State of Indiana. INPRS administers 16 funds including: Defined Benefit DB Fund • Public Employees’ Defined Benefit Account (PERF DB) • Teachers’ Pre-1996 Defined Benefit Account (TRF Pre-’96 DB) • Teachers’ 1996 Defined Benefit Account (TRF ’96 DB) • 1977 Police Officers’ and Firefighters’ Retirement Fund (’77 Fund) • Judges’ Retirement System (JRS) • Excise, Gaming and Conservation Officers’ Retirement Fund (EG&C) • Prosecuting Attorneys’ Retirement Fund (PARF) • Legislators’ Defined Benefit Fund (LE DB) Defined Contribution DC Fund • Public Employees’ Defined Contribution Account (PERF DC) • My Choice: Retirement Savings Plan for Public Employees (PERF MC DC) • Teachers’ Defined Contribution Account (TRF DC) • My Choice: Retirement Savings Plan for Teachers (TRF MC DC) • Legislators’ Defined Contribution Fund (LE DC) Other Postemployment Benefit / OPEB Fund • Special Death Benefit Fund (SDBF) • Retirement Medical Benefits Account Plan (RMBA) Custodial Fund • Local Public Safety Pension Relief Fund (LPSPR) For additional information regarding INPRS and the funds detailed above, please access: - 3 - [PDF page 4] http://www.in.gov/inprs/. For its’s defined benefit plan, INPRS has an assumed rate of return on invested assets of 6.25%. INPRS anticipates hiring two Core Plus Fixed Income managers, with a combined mandate size of approximately $185 million. It is expected that the chosen strategies will be benchmarked against the Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index. The mandates must meet guidelines outlined in INPRS’ Investment Policy Statement (https://www.in.gov/inprs/files/INPRS_IPS.pdf). The objective of this RFP is to identify those respondents with the expertise, track record, and resources to invest in the mandate described above. Required characteristics of the firm and/or portfolio managers: 1. Must be an investment advisor registered with the SEC. 2. Have existing Firm AUM of at least $2 billion. 3. The Firm must have a minimum track record of ten years acting as a manager of core plus fixed income. 4. The Firm and senior team must have a performance track record for managing core plus fixed income in compliance with Global Investment Performance Standards (“GIPS”) that extends at least seven years. 5. Have ability to offer a separate account structure or collective investment trust. All vehicle types will be considered; however, separate account mandates and collective investment trusts are generally preferred. a. Mandates that utilize a separate account structure will also require a Sudan and Terror States compliance screen as defined in IC 5-10.2-9 and IC 5-10.2-10 and Divestment Related to Boycott of, Divestment from, or Sanctions of Israel IC 5-10.2-11-1 and performed by a vendor on INPRS’ behalf. b. Mandates that utilize a separate account structure must agree to the use of INPRS’s forms and contracts for proposed separate accounts. 6. Utilize an active long-only management strategy that seeks to achieve higher risk-adjusted returns, net of fees, than the benchmark. 7. Have a minimum of at least $500 million in assets under management within the strategy. 8. Have a capacity of at least $100 million within the strategy. 9. Agree to comply with INPRS’ Investment Policy Statement, effective September 9, 2022, and as may be amended from time to time. Background: The INPRS DC Plan offers a custom, white-labeled Fixed Income Fund to participants as a separate core menu option and also serves as the fixed income component within the default Target Date Fund glidepath. The Fixed Income Fund is comprised of a passive Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index strategy representing 50% of the assets. The remaining 50% of the Fund is currently evenly split between two Core Plus Fixed Income managers. Additional Information: - 12 - [PDF page 13] The INPRS DC Plan leverages the extensive asset base and investment manager roster of the INPRS Defined Benefit Plan, where appropriate. Specifically, INPRS’ custodian (BNY Mellon) unitizes many investment manager mandates between the Defined Contribution Plan and the Defined Benefit Plan. Furthermore, INPRS negotiates favorable fee schedules with investment managers based on total assets across all mandates and plans.
