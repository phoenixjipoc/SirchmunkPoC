Introduction The San Diego City Employees' Retirement System (SDCERS) is requesting proposals from general investment consultants to provide comprehensive pension fund investment consulting services. SDCERS is seeking proposals from independent general investment consultants who have consulting experience in the public pension fund area. Firms are invited to participate in an evaluation process to further represent their capabilities of rendering the following consultant services: Investment Policy Asset Allocation and Portfolio Oversight Investment Manager Oversight Performance Measurement Training, Education and Client Service See section V. for further details on each service area. Proposal Participation, Completion and Submission Procedures General Investment Consulting firms wishing to respond to this RFP must meet the following minimum qualifications as of January 1, 2027: A. Mandatory Requirements Certification and Minimum Requirements SDCERS will accept proposals from firms that meet the minimum requirements listed below. The proposing firm must complete the Mandatory Requirements Certification (attached as Attachment A) and submit it with the cover letter. 1. The consulting firm must be a registered advisor under the Investment Company Act of 1940. Please include copies of ADV I and II (with schedules) as Exhibit A. 2. The consulting firm must have substantial experience advising multi-billion public pension plan retirement systems. 3. The consulting firm must have at least five (5) years of continuous experience in performing general investment consulting for large institutional clients (endowments and foundations), corporate pension plans or tax-exempt public funds. The consulting firm must not have, nor potentially have, a material conflict of interest to include, but not limited to: SDCERS’ Board, Staff, Actuary, External Auditor, Investment Managers, or other consultants. 9. The consulting firm must have its principal place of business within the United States and be subject to service of process in the United States. 11. All minimum qualifications must be met by January 1, 2027. B. Confidentiality All responses to this RFP become the property of SDCERS and will be kept confidential until a recommendation for award of a contract has been announced. Thereafter, submittals are subject to public inspection and disclosure under the law, including but not limited to, the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code §§ 7920.000 et seq.) In the event SDCERS receives a request for a copy or inspection of any proposal submitted pursuant to this RFP, SDCERS will notify the Proposer, the individual who is authorized to bind the consulting firm, before providing a response. There are specific exemptions to the Public SDCERS General Investment Consultant RFP – August 2026 Page 4 [PDF page 5] Records Act, however, it is the responsibility of the Proposer to assert and seek a judicial determination as to any right of confidentiality that may exist. SDCERS will not make that assertion on behalf of the Proposer. Absent a judicial determination that the documents are exempt from disclosure, they will be subject to public inspection. Submission of a proposal constitutes a complete waiver of any claims whatsoever against SDCERS, and/or its agents, Board Members, officers or employees, that SDCERS has violated a Proposer’s right to privacy, disclosed trade secrets or caused any damage by allowing the proposal to be inspected and/or copied. Insurance Requirements The selected firm shall provide evidence of Professional Liability insurance with contractual liability coverage covering the Proposer’s liability arising from errors and omissions (E&O) made directly or indirectly during the duration of the contract. The selected firm shall provide coverage for the total limits actually arranged by the selected firm but not less than a $10 million combined single limit, in addition to no less than $10 million of directors and officers (D&O) insurance. The selected firm shall maintain a Fidelity Bond covering employee dishonesty, in an amount arranged by the selected firm, but not less than $15 million, per occurrence. In addition, the selected firm shall maintain workers compensation coverage. Finally, the selected firm shall maintain cybersecurity insurance not less than $5 million. V.
