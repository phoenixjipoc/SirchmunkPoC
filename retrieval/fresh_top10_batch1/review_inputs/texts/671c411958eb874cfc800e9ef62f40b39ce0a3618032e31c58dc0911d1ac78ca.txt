RFP Objectives Missouri Local Government Employees Retirement System (LAGERS) is issuing this Request for Proposal (RFP) for the purpose of soliciting qualified firms to provide private market consulting services. A qualified Private Market Consultant will be capable of providing LAGERS with a variety of investment advisory, monitoring, and reporting services. The term ‘Private Market Consultant’ encompasses all private markets and alternative investment strategies including (but not limited to) private equity (venture capital, growth equity, and buyouts), private real asset (infrastructure, natural resources, and real estate), private debt (direct lending, mezzanine debt, and distressed debt), and other liquid alternatives (hedge funds). The selected consultant will be expected to offer analysis, advice, and recommendations with respect to LAGERS Private Market portfolio. As provided below, the RFP bifurcates year 1 services and year 2 onward, with LAGERS having the option (but not the obligation) to engage the Private Market Consultant with year 2 onward services. Overview of LAGERS LAGERS provides retirement and other benefits to the employees of more than 800 local government agencies and public entities in the state of Missouri. Established by the 74th Missouri General Assembly in 1967, LAGERS is administered under Chapter 70 of the Missouri Revised Statutes. LAGERS’ membership includes employees of many local government counties and cities, hospitals, libraries, ambulance districts, and other public entities. LAGERS has over 35,000 active members and pays retirement benefits to over 28,000 retirees and beneficiaries. For more information on LAGERS, Annual Comprehensive Financial Reports and other information can be viewed online at www.molagers.org. Overview of LAGERS’ Investments LAGERS manages, invests, and administers a defined benefit plan which paid out $455 million in retirement benefits in the fiscal year 2023. Evaluation Criteria Providers will be evaluated based on their written responses to this Proposal. Additional written information as requested will be considered against criteria that include: 1. Provider’s overall ability to provide the scope of services required by LAGERS. 2. Provider's depth of private market products and services, including LAGERS ability to access key research and professionals, including the client servicing team to be assigned to serve the LAGERS relationship. 3. Provider’s system and technology infrastructure used in the delivery of research and reporting and LAGERS ability to interface with existing systems and structures. 4. Provider’s ability to work with administrative and investment teams, providing investment support for pri- vate market structures, pacing, research, analysis, and training/education. 5. Provider’s ability to provide staff and Board of Trustees detailed reports, information, and discussion in development of action plans for current and future issues. 6. Breadth/depth of Provider’s clients, assets under advisement, industry reputation, and any potential con- flicts of interest between the Provider and LAGERS. 7. The overall quality of Provider’s written proposal and oral presentation, if any. 8. Provider’s fee proposal. 9. LAGERS’ internal and external costs of the transition. Fees and compensation will be important factors in the evaluation process; however, LAGERS will also evaluate on which Provider can service LAGERS’ needs in the most complete, efficient, and economic manner. LAGERS reserves the right to consider additional criteria not presented here. Miscellaneous Information Due Diligence Visits and Oral Presentations by Finalists Providers that submit proposals in response to this RFP may be required to accommodate an on-site visit by repre- sentatives of LAGERS or a presentation at the LAGERS office. Should this be required, LAGERS will determine the occurrence, date, and time of such visits. Quiet Time The Provider and its agents, officers, principals, and employees will not engage in any written or verbal communi- cation during the selection period between the Provider, or any individual assisting the Provider to be selected and the LAGERS Board of Trustees, or any LAGERS employee, whether or not such individual or employee is assisting in the selection of the Provider, regarding the merits of the Provider or regarding whether LAGERS should retain or select the Provider. The Provider will not engage in any lobbying efforts or other attempts to influence LAGERS, to be selected as the Provider. All such efforts made by the Provider, or its agents, officers, principals, employees, or lobbyists will be disclosed to the LAGERS Board of Trustees within five calendar days of becoming known. Violations of these Quiet Time provisions may result in disqualification.
