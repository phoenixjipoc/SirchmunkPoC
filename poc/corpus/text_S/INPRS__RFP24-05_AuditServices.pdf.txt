1.3    INPRS Background
       1.3.1   History

       INPRS was established by statute in 2011 as an independent body corporate and politic. The
       system is not a department or agency of the state but is an independent instrumentality exercising
       essential government functions. INPRS was established by legislation to manage the retirement
       funds of certain public employees throughout the State of Indiana. INPRS administers 16 funds
       including:
          Defined Benefit DB Fund
              • Public Employees’ Defined Benefit Account (PERF DB)
              • Teachers’ Pre-1996 Defined Benefit Account (TRF Pre-’96 DB)
              • Teachers’ 1996 Defined Benefit Account (TRF ’96 DB)
              • 1977 Police Officers’ and Firefighters’ Retirement Fund (’77 Fund)
              • Judges’ Retirement System (JRS)
              • Excise, Gaming and Conservation Officers’ Retirement Fund (EG&C)
              • Prosecuting Attorneys’ Retirement Fund (PARF)
              • Legislators’ Defined Benefit Fund (LE DB)
          Defined Contribution DC Fund
              • Public Employees’ Defined Contribution Account (PERF DC)
              • My Choice: Retirement Savings Plan for Public Employees (PERF MC DC)
              • Teachers’ Defined Contribution Account (TRF DC)
              • My Choice: Retirement Savings Plan for Teachers (TRF MC DC)
              • Legislators’ Defined Contribution Fund (LE DC)
          Other Postemployment Benefit / OPEB Fund
             • Special Death Benefit Fund (SDBF)
             • Retirement Medical Benefits Account Plan (RMBA)
           Custodial Fund
             • Local Public Safety Pension Relief Fund (LPSPR)

        1.3.2    Audit Background Information

        INPRS’s Internal Audit Department consists of four full-time employees including the Chief

        authority and responsibility of such department are set forth in the Internal Audit Department
        Charter, set forth in Appendix B of the Board Governance Manual available at:

                http://www.in.gov/inprs/files/BoardGovernanceManualINPRS.pdf

        RSM has provided external audit services to INPRS since 2014. INPRS’s procurement policy in
        the INPRS Board Governance Manual limits the contract length to 10 years and requires
        solicitation of new bids a maximum of every 10 years.

        Prior to 2014, the Indiana State Board of Accounts (SBOA) performed annual external audits.
        SBOA currently has oversight responsibilities for the State and Quasi Agency (including INPRS)
        audits.

SECTION 3 – SCOPE OF SERVICES

Introduction

Indiana Public Retirement System (INPRS) is seeking professional assistance in conducting the annual
audits of our financial statements and maintaining and instituting compliance with the new Governmental
Accounting Standards Board (GASB) Pension Standards.

The vendor awarded the Audit Services engagement will be responsible for providing the major
components below:

3.1 Services Required – Financial Statements

  1. Perform an annual audit of INPRS’s financial statements in accordance with audit standards
     generally accepted in the United States of America as well as in accordance with Indiana law and
     GASB. Express an opinion as to whether the comparative financial statements present fairly in
     all material respects, the plan net assets of INPRS as of the applicable June 30, 20XX and 20XX
     and changes in plan net assets for the years then ended in conformity with U.S. generally
     accepted accounting principles. The audit shall include tests of the accounting and other
     supporting records as deemed necessary by the independent auditor in order to express such
     opinion, including any required testing of the underlying census data of INPRS’s members. In
     addition, express an opinion on the Other Supplementary Schedules included in the Annual
     Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR) Financial Section and perform limited procedures on
     the Required Supplementary Information included in the ACFR.

  2. INPRS currently consists of four multi-employer cost-sharing defined benefit pension plans, four
     single employer defined benefit pension plans, five defined contribution plans, two other
     employment benefit plans and an investment trust fund.


                                                  - 12 -
  3. Address completed reports to the INPRS Board of Trustees.

  4. Complete the audits and issue the audit reports by November 30 of each year as of INPRS’s June
     30 fiscal year end. The partner-in-charge of the engagement will give an annual oral presentation
     to the INPRS Board of Trustees on the results of the audit.

  5. Keep INPRS informed of all current events affecting the public retirement industry, including an
     analysis of proposed accounting standards and federal tax matters. Furthermore, INPRS will
     have an occasional need to consult with the independent auditor’s technical specialists to solve
     technical and work-related issues.

  6. The independent auditor and INPRS acknowledge the oversight responsibilities of the Indiana
     State Board of Accounts (SBOA) for the State and Quasi Agency audits. The selected firm will
     follow the minimum audit requirements as presented in the “Uniform Compliance Guidelines For
     Audit Of Hospitals And State And Local Governments By Authorized Independent Public
     Accountants” (available at: https://www.in.gov/sboa/library/private-examiner-manuals/uniform-
     compliance-guidelines-for-audit-of-hospitals-and-state-and-local-governments ) (“Guidelines for
     Audit”).

  7. The SBOA State Examiner will be notified immediately if there is a misappropriation of funds
     that is suspected to be the result of malfeasance, misfeasance, or nonfeasance discovered during
     the audit process.

  8. Along with providing findings of noncompliance required to be included for the financial and
     federal audits, the independent auditor will perform any required review of compliance for laws,
     regulations, and the “Accounting and Uniform Compliance Guidelines Manual for State and
     Quasi Agencies” located at (https://www.in.gov/sboa/library/unit-specific-uniform-compliance-
     guidelines/state-agencies/ ) and as stated in the Guidelines for Audit.

  9. The SBOA will be notified of the date, time, and place that the results of the audit will be
     discussed with INPRS. The notification will take place at least two days prior to the
     meeting. Within ten business days of the issuance of the audit report, the independent auditor
     will transmit by email an Adobe Acrobat PDF copy of the audited financial statements,
     supplemental audit report, and any other audit communications to the Indiana State Board of
     Accounts.

  10. INPRS may from time to time require additional services beyond the above scope and requests a
      separate hourly rate schedule for these purposes. Should INPRS require such additional services,
      a formal statement of work will be developed and the rate schedule you propose will be used as
      the basis for compensation. The request for completion of this schedule, however, does not
      represent an intention or commitment by INPRS to grant exclusive right to the successful
      proposer to provide said additional services.

     (PERF), Teachers’ Retirement Fund Pre 1996 Account (TRF Pre 1996), Teachers’ Retirement
     Fund Post 1996 Account (TRF 1996) and the 1977 Fund for the year ended June 30, 2025, as well
     as the related Schedule of Non-Employer Allocation, related Schedule of Employer Pension

  3. Address completed reports to the INPRS Board of Trustees.

  4. Complete the audits and issue the audit reports of the GASB 68 schedules by January 31 of each
     year as of INPRS’s June 30 fiscal year end.



SECTION 4 – CONTRACT AWARD
