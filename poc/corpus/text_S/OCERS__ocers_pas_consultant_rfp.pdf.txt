Section 1: Introduction
The Orange County Employees Retirement System (“OCERS”) is requesting proposals from qualified
firms interested in providing consulting services to aid in the design, selection of, and smooth
implementation of their new Pension Administration Solution (PAS). This solution will replace their
current system, V3. The selected consultant will be responsible for providing Oversight Project
Management services, which will include staff support and quality assurance. These services are vital to
support the successful implementation of the new PAS. It's worth noting that the selection of the new
PAS will be made independently of, but simultaneously with, the selection of the implementation
consultant.

The project's expected start date is approximately February 2024, and the entire PAS implementation
process is estimated to take around 3 years to complete. OCERS envisions this as a full-time consulting
engagement.

The primary role of the selected consultant will be to assist OCERS in efficiently managing and
overseeing the implementation process. This involves active participation, careful review, facilitation,
and guidance throughout the project. The consultant's goal is to ensure that the new system fulfills all
project requirements, is completed within the designated timeframe, and remains within the allocated
budget. The solution may include multiple software solutions that will need to interact with each other.
Our intention is to have a future proof solution with the ability to grow and incorporate current and
emerging technologies such as Intelligent Automation.

Section 2: Background
OCERS was established in 1945 under the County Employees Retirement Law of 1937, providing
members with retirement, disability, death, and cost-of-living benefits. There are approximately 50,000
members served by OCERS, of which over 19,000 are retirees. OCERS is governed by a nine-member
Board of Retirement (“Board”), which has plenary authority and fiduciary responsibility for the
investment of moneys and administration of the retirement system. OCERS has over one hundred
employees, and the Board appoints a Chief Executive Officer responsible for the agency’s management.
For additional information about OCERS, please refer to the OCERS website at ocers.org.

 Pension Administration Project Oversight

 OCERS is seeking an implementation partner for this mission critical pension
 administration project. The service required consists of developing and reviewing key

 addressed:

    1.    Planning – Coordinating tasks associated with requirements definition, IT strategic

          and implementation activities. Ensure that the software vendor(s) satisfies all

          item as stated. Coordinate with OCERS’ Enterprise Project Management Office
          (EPMO).

          followed throughout the system development life cycle.

    6.    Project Deliverables – Provide project management reports and metrics for

          comment on all project deliverables, documentation, etc., e.g.:
             •           Perform a needs and requirements assessment
             •           Create a staffing plan
             •           Create an IT Strategic Roadmap plan
             •           Manage the project budget
             •           Coordinate and manage vendors

             •           Coordination of the following:
                                 •           Statement of Work and Task Descriptions
                                 •           Data conversion plans and reconciliation procedures
                                 •          System test plans, scripts, cases, etc.
                                 •          User acceptance plan, acceptance criteria, etc.
                                 •          Project Schedule including cutover plans: timelines
                                            and process
