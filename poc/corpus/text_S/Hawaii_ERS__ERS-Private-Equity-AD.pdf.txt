          STATE OF HAWAII, EMPLOYEES’ RETIREMENT SYSTEM
                   REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)
                  FOR PRIVATE EQUITY CONSULTING
                     AND INVESTMENT SERVICES


The Employees’ Retirement System of the State of Hawaii (ERS) is soliciting
proposals for qualified firm(s) to provide private equity investment consulting and
investment management services to the ERS. The consulting firm(s) must be
United States based, registered under the Investment Advisors Act of 1940, been
in business for a minimum of five years and have at least one defined benefit
public pension fund client with assets greater than $3 billion. The primary
consultant to be assigned to the ERS must have a minimum of seven years
experience providing domestic and international investment consulting and
management services to public pension plans.

The consulting firm will have discretion with respect to certain investment
decisions regarding a portion of the Assets.

The RFP may be obtained by calling (808) 5861705 or downloaded from the
ERS website: http://ers.ehawaii.gov/resources/procurement starting at 9 a.m.
(Hawaii Standard Time) on October 3, 2017. The RFP will also be available
online at www.spo.hawaii.gov.org
