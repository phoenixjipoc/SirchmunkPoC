               SECTION I: INTRODUCTORY SECTION


  The City Treasurer’s Office of Hartford, Connecticut is soliciting proposals for a full-service
  professional  investment  consulting  firm  with broad  investment  expertise and  service
  capabilities to work alongside with and assist operationally with the City Treasurer’s Office.
  The City Treasurer’s Office will be contracting with only one investment consulting firm to assist
  with the management of the MERF and OPEB.


1. INTRODUCTION & BACKGROUND
 PLEASE NOTE: This section contains important background information regarding the City
  Treasurer’s Office’s Municipal Employees’ Retirement Fund (the “MERF”) the Other Post-
  Employment Benefits Trust Fund (“OPEB” or the “Trust”) and the proposal process for
  selecting a General Investment Consultant.

     The MERF is a contributory defined benefit local government retirement plan. Under the
      Charter and Code of the City of Hartford, the elected City Treasurer and City of Hartford
      Pension Commission (the “Pension Commission”) are the fiduciaries of the MERF.

     The City established the OPEB Committee to oversee the investment of Trust assets, and
      pursuant to The Hartford Charter, the City designated the duty of Trustee of the Trust to
      the elected City Treasurer.  City Ordinance established the OPEB Committee, which
       consists of the members of the City Treasurer’s Office, the Pension Commission, including
      the employee representative, together with the Mayor, and the Superintendent of Schools.
     The OPEB Committee and the Trustee are fiduciaries of the Trust.

     The members of the Pension Commission and OPEB Committee, in conjunction with the
       elected City Treasurer determine policies and make investment decisions concerning the
       administrative and investment operations of the MERF and OPEB. The City Treasurer,
        staff, and general consultant will work with the Pension Commission on matters related to
      asset allocation, performance management, investment policy, investment manager
      searches and oversight, and other matters as may be required.


         1.1   Asset Allocation:

         Information  relating  to  the MERF  and OPEB,  including  investment  policies,
        performance, staffing and results can be found on the City Treasurer’s website:

         https://www.hartfordcitytreasurer.org

2. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

  A proposing firm (Proposer) shall meet all of the following minimum qualifications to the City
  Treasurer’s Office’s satisfaction to be given further consideration:

   •  The Proposer shall have been in business for at least ten (10) years providing
     general consulting services.

   •  The senior members of the Proposer (those in top leadership positions) shall have worked
      together for at least three (3) years.

   •  The Proposer shall not subcontract the management of the account, and  all
     personnel responsible for the account must be employees of the Proposer.

   •  The Proposer shall currently serve as consultant for at least five public pension funds
     each with over $1.5 billion in assets.

   •  The primary consultant assigned to the MERF and OPEB relationship shall have a
     minimum of five (5) years of consulting experience with defined benefit public pension
     plans and have been employed by the Proposer for at least three (3) years.

   •  The Proposer shall serve as a fiduciary to the MERF and OPEB and shall
     acknowledge in writing its compliance with the MERF’s and OPEB’s investment
      policies and guidelines.

   •  The Proposer shall not have existing or potential material conflicts of interests to the
      City Treasurer’s Office and staff, the Pension Commission, the OPEB Committee, their
      actuary, auditor, investment managers, or other service providers.

   •  Proposer shall agree to execute a contract similar in form to that presented in Appendix E, Form
      of Contract.

   •  The Proposer shall maintain a current registration with the Securities and
     Exchange Commission as an Advisor under the Investment Advisory Act of 1940
     as amended.

   •  The Proposer shall carry the following minimum insurance coverage or must have
      applied for  it by the RFP submission date:

         a.  General Liability                              Min     $ 5,000,000
         b.  Errors and Omissions (Professional Liability)       Min     $ 5,000,000
         c.  Fiduciary Liability                              Min     $ 1,000,000
         d.  Workers’ Compensation and Employer’s Liability   Min     $ 1,000,000
         e.  Cybersecurity Coverage                        Min     $ 1,000,000

   The City Treasurer’s Office reserves the right to require a higher insurance coverage.

3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

  Under the direction of the City Treasurer and her designated staff, the consultant shall provide
  comprehensive full-retainer, general investment consulting advice and services.

   Specifically, the successful firm will demonstrate superior experience and capability in the
  areas of investment policy development and compliance, investment manager research and
   selection, asset allocation, risk management and Trustee education.


  The following are examples of the duties the City Treasurer’s Office expects the general
  investment consultant to perform.

       3.1    Policy Duties:

       •  Annually, review the Investment Policy Statements ("IPS")

       • Recommend and assist in drafting changes to the IPS;

       •  Assist in establishing and/or revising asset class level manager guidelines and
           restrictions;

       •  Annually, review and recommend appropriate benchmark revisions;

       •  Annually, provide capital market assumptions and expected returns to model asset
          allocation updates;

       •  Assist in establishing systems and procedures for monitoring policies on an ongoing
          basis;

       •  Provide recommendations concerning long-term investment policy, investment
          objectives and strategy, including manager continuation policies and rebalancing
           policies;

       •  Provide recommendations concerning investment goals, policies, guidelines
        and procedures for private and publicly managed portfolios;

       •  Provide recommendations concerning risk management policies, and other
          related issues;

       •  Provide compliance monitoring.

•  Conduct periodic asset allocation and asset-liability studies;

•  Conduct annual asset allocation reviews and provide corresponding asset allocation
   recommendations addressing relevant risk and return parameters;

•  Provide an annual liquidity review based on portfolio investment allocations and
   projected cash flows;

•  Provide recommendations concerning investment manager allocation and structure;

The City Treasurer’s Office requests that response to this RFP be as succinct as possible

4.3      Restrictions

•   Respondent Communications

 Questions and requests for clarification relevant to this RFP shall be submitted in writing
 during the time frame defined above. Assistance will be available to firms seeking
  clarification to questions or comments stated in this RFP.

•  Contact with Pension Commission and OPEB Committee Members is Prohibited

 During the Consultant search, a prospective provider of such services shall not have
 any contact with members of the Pension Commission and OPEB Committee. To ensure
 fairness, after the RFP is issued, the Pension Commission and OPEB Committee shall be
 prohibited from responding to any inquiries or discussions with interested consulting
 firms where there is a potential for bias or favoritism or appearance of impropriety due to
 personal or potential conflict of interest.

 Monthly Pension Commission and OPEB Committee meetings are excluded from this
  restriction.

 After the Q&A period, City Treasurer and staff will also be prohibited from responding to
 any inquiries or discussions with interested firms where there is a potential for bias or
 favoritism or appearance of impropriety due to personal or potential conflict of interest.

 Contact with the Pension Commission and OPEB Committee or staff within the prohibited
 period may lead to disqualification.

5. PROPOSAL EVALUATION CRITERIA & PROCESS

  Fees and compensation will be an important factor in the evaluation of investment consulting
  proposals. However,the City Treasurer’s Office, the OPEB Committee and the Pension
  Commission are not required to award the contract to the lowest cost bidder. Selection of
   finalists and determinations of compliance with minimum qualifications is solely and
   exclusively within the judgment of the Pension Commission and OPEB Committee based on
  the recommendation of the City Treasurer, and may be made solely on the basis of their
  evaluation of a candidate’s suitability.

  The following is a summary of some of the general considerations that will be used to
  determine the firms that will be selected as finalists:

             •  Quality and objectivity of investment advice
             •  Understanding of the needs of the MERF and OPEB & ability to deliver
             •  Firm experience & client relevant experience
             •   Analytical tools, research & process
             •  Firm personnel & proposed consultant team
             •   Client & industry feedback
             •  Firm’s diversity, equity and inclusion (“DEI”) policies and related materials
