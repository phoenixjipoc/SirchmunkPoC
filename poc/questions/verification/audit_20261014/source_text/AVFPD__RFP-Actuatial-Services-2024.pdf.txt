GENERAL INFORMATION
The Apple Valley Fire Protection District (AVFPD) is seeking proposals from qualified
professional actuary firms (Actuary) to provide services to value Other Post-Employment
Benefits (OPEB) in compliance with Governmental Accounting Standards Board Statement
No. 75 (GASB 75) for inclusion in AVFPD’s Comprehensive Annual Financial Report as of
June 30, 2024, and beyond.

Said valuations must be performed in accordance with generally accepted actuarial
standards of practice promulgated by the Actuarial Standards Board and based on the
actuarial assumptions and methods prescribed by the California Public Employees’
Retirement System (CalPERS) Board. The required services and performance conditions
are described in the Scope of Work.

There is no expressed or implied obligation of AVFPD to reimburse responding firms for
any expense incurred in preparing proposals in response to this request. During the
evaluation process, the AVFPD reserves the right, where  it may serve AVFPD’s best
interest, to request additional information or clarifications from proposers, or to allow
corrections of errors or omissions.

AVFPD reserves the right to retain all proposals submitted and to use any ideas in a
proposal regardless of whether that proposal  is selected. Submission of a proposal
indicates acceptance by the firm of the conditions contained in this request for proposal,
unless clearly and specifically noted in the proposal submitted and confirmed in the
contract between AVFPD and the firm selected.

To be considered, a proposal must be received by 11:59 PM, July 31, 2024. Please see the
Instructions to Submitting Firms in this document for further information.

BACKGROUND

The Apple Valley Fire Protection District was formed in 1951 and was incorporated as a
Fire District in 1962 under the California Health and Safety Code, Local Fire District Law of
1961. AVFPD is located approximately 90 miles northeast of Los Angeles and provides fire
service to a population of approximately 94,000 residents within a service area of
approximately 206 square miles. AVFPD employs 59 full-time employees. AVFPD’s
Adopted Budget for fiscal year 2024 totals $15.5 million.

AVFPD contracts with the California Public Employees’ Retirement System to provide

a monthly health insurance reimbursement to retirees up to a maximum contribution set
by AVFPD’s Board of Directors, based on years of service and position held. As of
December 1, 2007, the plan is closed to new employees, excluding Firer Chief, Deputy Fire
Chief, Finance Officer, Division Chief, Battalion Chief, Fire Marshal, Deputy Fire Marshal,
Fleet Services Manager and Administrative Services Manager.

SCOPE OF WORK

The Apple Valley Fire Protection  District  is seeking proposals from qualified firms
interested in providing actuarial services to AVFPD for valuation of OPEB offered by
AVFPD. The Actuary must at a minimum:

    1.  Prepare actuarial valuations in compliance with the reporting requirements of all
       applicable GASB statements, including but not limited to, GASB Statements 74 and
      75 and any future GASB pronouncements and governmental accounting reporting
      requirements.

    2.  Deliver OPEB related biennial valuations as of June 30, 2024, June 30, 2026, and
      June 30, 2028.

    3.  Deliver OPEB biennial roll-forward updates as of June 30, 2025, June 30, 2027, and
      June 30, 2029.

    4. Recommended contributions/plan funding for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025 – 2030.

    5.  Prepare necessary information for inclusion in AVFPD’s Comprehensive Annual
       Financial Report to comply with current and future GASB pronouncement reporting
     and disclosure requirements.

    6.  Provide  all documents and information (including the actuarial  certification,
      funding policy certification, and Excel valuation information spreadsheet) required
      by CalPERS for agencies funding with the California Employers’’ Retiree Benefit
       Trust (CERBT) along with a certified final valuation results outline.

    7. Meet with AVFPD in person or virtually as needed, to make necessary decisions
     and report progress on valuations, including auditor related inquiries and financial
       reporting issues.

    8.  Assistance  in implementing any new GASB statements and other  financial
      pronouncements  related  to  OPEB  and  providing  ongoing  professional
       consultation.

    9. Working Paper Retention - All working papers and reports must be retained, at the
       Actuary's expense, for a minimum of seven (7) years after the last year of the
       contract, unless the Actuary is notified in writing by AVFPD of the need to extend
      the retention period. The Actuary will be required to make working papers available
     upon request, including, but not limited to, AVFPD or its designees. The Actuary
        shall work with AVFPD staff and financial auditors as needed to comply with GASB
      75 requirements and Comprehensive Annual Financial Report preparation and
       audits.

   10. The Actuary shall respond to the reasonable inquiries of successor Actuaries and
       allow successor Actuaries to review working papers  relating to matters of
      continuing GASB and accounting significance, if needed.

   11. AVFPD shall be free to publish the valuations and reports as they see fit without
      obtaining prior permission from the Actuary as long as they are not used in a
       potentially misleading manner and no material subsequent event has occurred that
      might render the valuation and reports potentially misleading.

   12. The proposal package shall present all-inclusive actuarial fees for each year of the
       contract term. Actuarial fees associated with optional extensions exercised by
     AVFPD will be negotiated at the time of contract extension. AVFPD requires the
       total costs be stated as a “not to exceed” basis and shall be inclusive of labor, travel,
       report preparation, printing, and all other expenses incurred by the Actuary.
